# Photometry helper scientific checks

Claim: fixed raw torchgaiaxp BP/RP photometric matrices map G-normalised coefficients to fluxes on the same scale as observed survey flux divided by `10**((8.5-G)/2.5)`; training-calibrated Huber flux supervision propagates only through hidden XP predictions.

Implementation: `xp_photometry.py`, raw SDSS/PanSTARRS1/Sky_Mapper g/r/i matrices, no additive standardisation offsets. Training-only median synthetic-minus-observed flux residual and robust MAD scatter are in per-band median observed flux units. Scatter lower bound is 0.02, dimensionless. The fitted scatter includes measurement noise; adding available magnitude uncertainty in quadrature is conservative. Missing/nonfinite magnitude uncertainties fall back to the calibrated scatter.

Evidence: 128 seeded random full BP/RP coefficient vectors compare the flattened projection against actual `torchgaiaxp.generate(XPSpectra(...))` band-wise contraction, maximum absolute difference 8.758115402030107e-47. `photometry-projection.json` preserves package git/source-asset hashes, columns, bands and zero points. Focused tests independently evaluate NumPy standardized residual/Huber math, hidden-only gradients, physical flux-unit scaling by 1e-24, missing observations, incomplete XP, nonfinite scored predictions, detached decoder targets, count minimum and fixed global microbatch denominator.

Runner audit: target coefficients are unscaled into G-normalised units before training calibration; apparent survey magnitudes use the combined exponent `-0.4*(mag-zp+8.5-G)`; errors are converted from scaled magnitude units; hybrid observed-visible/predicted-hidden coefficients are projected. Decoder photometry targets are detached and restricted to hidden observed-finite photometric columns. True G is used as a supervised-target normalisation only. The coefficient auxiliary denominator remains the common scored-entry count. No covariance or physical magnitude logarithm is needed in training.
