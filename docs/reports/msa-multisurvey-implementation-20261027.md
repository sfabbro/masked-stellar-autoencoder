# Multi-survey implementation and six-target spline pilot

This implementation is an intermediate engineering milestone for the user's `docs/masked_stellar_autoencoder_torchregress_final_plan.md`, not a calibrated stellar catalogue or evidence that any head wins. The old remediation jobs remain pinned to their original MSA and torchregress commits. The immutable pilot source and experiment lock use torchregress `906a1c17639dcbf308fdb835270701bbc04f91e0`. The current MSA and CANFAR development locks now pin `82ca1996cc4f3ca0be3e730d38a99db482a79378`, which merges that work with the later torchregress main `023a9ec51f7fb64e513351bd0db0464b5382ef5d`. No completed pilot is claimed to have used this later merge. Its declared requirements move NumPy to at least 2.5.4, SciPy to at least 1.18.1 and PyTorch to at least 2.14.1. Both locks were resolved and checked without installing into the existing environments.

The new reusable library provides a shared-latent Gaussian marginal likelihood, normalized small-block outlier mixtures, zero-sum relative survey splines, independently scoped common and quality-condition maps, and nonlinear one-dimensional forward-observation quadrature. These primitives are tested on synthetic cases and do not establish survey calibration or physical identifiability.

The MSA prototype includes a sparse source-indexed survey reader, a digest-verified external-link sidecar overlay, a three-target full-covariance canonical atmospheric head and a joint observation objective, plus a signed-parallax likelihood adapter. The supervised path accepts natural input availability and adds no XP mask. Reconstruction is a separate, explicitly FIT-role and source-bound stream. An anchor declaration must be content-, source-, registry- and split-bound before a common correction is enabled. Such checks cannot certify anchor independence or coverage: those require a real, separately audited manifest. The code currently relies on upstream split and masking validation and is not yet an end-to-end production trainer.

The DESI DR1 source-only adapter reads the existing publisher-hash-bound FITS, audits extension/sky associations, retains repeated Gaia identities and native row provenance, and never silently converts global metallicity to Fe/H. It retains finite usable parameter values with unknown/nonpositive errors as unknown rather than zero; the raw errors are preserved and the fitting likelihood requires a declared positive floor. The selection is an explicit quality subset, not all DESI measurements. It uses the raw RVS FeH scale; the published temperature/gravity-dependent correction is not silently applied. No catalogue has been added to the training source by this code alone; full feature joins, dependency resolution for repeated spectra, leakage-safe splits and survey/anchor assessments remain required.

The existing unrestricted coupled 6D NSF runs `bpw6joq8` and `hx177dr5` reached 1,000 updates but failed the selected-head sparse-label integration gate. The candidate alternative is a 6D Gaussian-copula rational-quadratic spline flow: it produces coupled whole-vector draws and has exact intrinsic marginals under any missing-label pattern, while its Gaussian copula is less expressive than the unrestricted NSF. Quoted-label-error convolution remains finite-QMC and is subject to the unchanged initial and selected-head gates; unknown errors are not claimed to be measured zeros. Synthetic Gaussian, non-linear numerical-integration, sampling, gradient and precision tests passed. A copy-only actual-device preflight and actual gate checks are required in every pilot. A successful preflight is not a physical-correlation or six-dimensional ground-truth certificate.

The original benchmark masks, known ancestry exclusions and native reference definitions remain separate. In particular the current atmosphere reader does not claim that `[M/H]` equals `[Fe/H]` or that the various alpha definitions are interchangeable; age and distance teacher products remain distinct from independent physical truth. Full multi-survey ingestion, calibrated anchors, a production fit/evaluation pipeline and physically supported covariance remain unfinished.

### Follow-up: synthetic consensus loop and source identity guards

The star-index reader checks its source file's identity before and after digesting, checks again before access, isolates its registry from external mutation and rejects changes to the pinned registry. These file-stat guards detect ordinary mutation; they are not an adversarial lock. The new group split propagates declared exclusions across transitive cluster/family groups and is deterministic under input order. It cannot discover unprovided ancestors or groups; assignments must be frozen before fitting.

The `ConsensusTrainer` prototype rebuilds supervised FIT batches from the pinned reader, keeps their natural XP inputs, and constructs a separate reconstruction mask. It records source/registry/FIT IDs, head and survey-gauge buffers/settings, declared noise floors, optimizer parameter order, selected code revisions and caller-provided parent/normalization hashes. It supports an exclusive new checkpoint and restores the optimizer and RNG state. Parent and normalization hashes are declarations rather than verification that those files were actually loaded; sampling/scheduler state, throughput, independent anchors, real feature joins and actual CANFAR integration remain the caller's responsibility. No physical-scale certificate, fully production-ready trainer or training against the new DESI sidecar is claimed.

Two six-target Gaussian-copula spline pilots have now completed their selected integration checks; the third failed. The [saved-array report and plots](msa-remediation-copula6-20261027/index.md) records the outcomes and a clear remaining failure on a metal-poor development star. This does not choose a winner or establish physical covariance.

Existing role locks can be supplied for candidate stars, including separate `devtest` and `external` roles. Their roles propagate to declared linked groups; conflicting fixed roles in a group are rejected. A declared exclusion takes precedence and reports how many fixed roles were overridden. Unprovided memberships and incomplete ancestral exposure remain outside the certificate.

### DESI-to-cache identity census

A distinct source-only census is prepared to compare every quality-selected DESI identity to the existing remediation source roles and the 150-million-row input cache. It retains all DESI duplicate-row counts and per-target availability, checks the complete cached ID file hash, samples raw-source/cache feature associations, and writes identity-level membership plus aggregate counts. Cache feature contents receive bounded association checks rather than a full-content hash. An absent cache match is not proof that a star lacks Gaia XP or features elsewhere; outside-base role and ancestor exposure remain unknown. The output does not resolve dependent repeated spectra, assign new FIT/TEST roles, train a model or certify physical truth. Its real counts are unavailable until the accepted job actually completes.

### DESI unique-spectrum overlay

A source-only overlay is prepared for the existing remediation source. It selects the DESI row with highest native `SN_R` per Gaia identity, breaking exact ties by original source-row number, without inspecting target values or model predictions. The original full raw sidecar, including duplicate observations, remains intact and SHA-bound. The overlay retains the base's feature rows and existing FIT/TUNE/CAL/devtest/external/excluded roles verbatim, while linking the selected DESI native observations. It reports the exact overlap between existing logg labels and selected DESI labels within each role. It does not assign new roles to outside-base stars, validate DESI errors or scale, model cross-survey dependencies, or train a model. The policy of selecting one spectrum is an explicit baseline choice, not proof that repeated spectra are independent or interchangeable.

The sparse collator still rejects repeated or dependent observations by default. An explicit `lowest_source_row` baseline may select one record only when repeated records come from the same source; it records the kept and discarded source-row IDs and never examines target values. Different sources sharing a dependency still fail closed. The FIT gate and checkpoint contract bind the chosen policy and recheck the recorded selection. This is separate from the DESI overlay's higher-S/N selection and is not a statistical independence or representativeness claim.

The canonical head can run in float64 while the pretrained encoder and reconstruction path remain float32; the latent cast preserves gradients to the encoder. This permits the marginalized likelihood to handle mixed Kelvin/dex scales with higher numerical precision, but it does not by itself prove conditioning on real catalog batches or on CUDA.

### CUDA consensus preflight

A separate two-update CANFAR preflight is prepared against the verified DESI/APOGEE/GALAH overlay and the existing large-model checkpoint. It checks the actual FIT source bindings, per-survey presence, unmasked supervised input, separately masked reconstruction, finite supervised encoder gradients and reconstruction decoder gradients. The head and observation likelihood use float64; the pretrained encoder and decoder remain in their original precision. The illustrative error floors are explicitly `[100 K, 0.1 dex, 0.1 dex]` for each source and are not scientifically calibrated. This is a small numerical preflight with no saved model weights, not an epoch, an experiment result or evidence of physically valid cross-survey covariance.

The accepted preflight subsequently passed on CUDA: two finite-gradient updates on 12 FIT stars, natural supervised XP and separately masked reconstruction, with no model weights saved. See the [completion receipt](../../experiments/xp/multisurvey-consensus-preflight-completion-20261028.json). The test does not certify throughput, survey scale, error floors, physical anchors or a trained model.

### FIT union and source-stratified sampling

The verified sparse reader operates on the union of registered sources and preserves per-target masks; it does not require survey intersections. A source-only census is prepared to measure the exact FIT availability after repeat resolution, including label loss from discarded repeats. The deterministic star sampler supports complete survey-presence strata and inverse-allocation weights for the eligible-star mean; its sampled IDs, stratum mapping, weights and RNG state are validated. The caller must bind these checks to the trainer and treat reconstruction sampling separately. These primitives do not yet constitute an end-to-end fit.

The current overlay has APOGEE, GALAH and DESI, not Gaia GSP-Spec. A potential Gaia RVS file in the project allocation is being inspected; its contents, quality flags and overlap remain unverified. See the [FIT label audit](msa-desi-fit-source-audit-20261028.md) for the gravity-scale differences that make naive pooling unsafe.
