Joint six-target NSF pilots: recovered evaluation

Three 1,000-update pilots trained an unrestricted joint six-dimensional NSF using a sample-based energy objective on the existing pooled six-target source. The fine-tuned reconstruction variant adds the original masked reconstruction loss. The models retain natural XP for supervision. All three original runs failed with GPU memory exhaustion after training; the figures come from separately submitted inference-only recoveries using their selected checkpoints, with reduced batch and sampling chunks.

Limits: this is a previously used development panel, not an untouched test or a same-star Andrae comparison. The metal-poor panel has only five Fe/H references, four logg, no alpha, one age and no distance reference. Age and distance references are model-dependent catalogue posteriors; alpha is on its source scale. Observed PIT is unavailable where measurement errors are unknown. The corner plots show actual joint samples; nonzero covariance does not prove physically calibrated correlations. The unrestricted positive age transform also permits unphysical ages: some saved samples exceed 40 Gyr.

One selected star, Gaia 4926812820427520, has catalogue [Fe/H] = −2.175 and logg = 1.257, while the reconstruction model’s sample medians are −0.236 and 3.145. This is a substantial discrepancy; its cause has not been isolated.

The forthcoming DR12 source jobs are separate and have not contributed labels to these pilots. The older unrestricted NSF marginal-likelihood integration failures remain unresolved; this sample-based energy loss does not certify those NLL calculations.
