Predictions are not six-target physical posteriors. These selected stars have eligible Teff/logg/FeH labels, but no eligible alpha/age/distance references in this crossmatch. Gaussian and scalar NSF use independent conditional coordinates; MDN shares a mixture component with limited sparse pair-label support. Alpha at FeH below−2 is diagnostic. Age/distance training targets are StarHorse posterior medians. Red denotes an eligible measured-label reference/error; gold would denote a catalogue-posterior span. Contours approximate68/95% predictive mass in plotted coordinates and are not conformal guarantees.

Models chosen by TUNE-only macro observed marginal NLL within each family: Gaussian CECDF seed50, MDN ranking seed50, scalar NSF NLL+0.1 contrastive seed50. Different auxiliary objectives, same short-screen reconstruction budget. This is a representative comparison rather than a universal winner claim.
