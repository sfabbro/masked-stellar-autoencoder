Final large-sample six-target review

Completed October 25, 2026 (America/Vancouver). Four fixed recipes, 4,412,982 feature-eligible evaluation identities. This is a review of saved predictions against source-qualified catalogue references.

Monitoring has stopped: the scheduled task was deleted; no task-owned active CANFAR sessions or local dispatchers/collectors/report servers remain. All outputs are saved. Shutdown evidence

What the completed comparison supports

Keep Gaussian and noise-aware NSF as the two useful candidates; the mixed contrastive NSF remains a tail-focused alternative. Gaussian has the lowest global XP log g, Fe/H, age and distance MAE in this four-arm comparison; NSF has the lowest Teff and alpha MAE. Mixed improves metal-poor XP Fe/H MAE from 0.2286 to 0.2198 dex versus matched NSF, while worsening global Fe/H and alpha errors. This is a descriptive held-out comparison, not a new TEST-driven model promotion or causal multi-seed claim.

Distribution calibration is not ready for final scientific delivery. The nominal intrinsic 90% intervals under-cover reported references substantially, especially alpha. Intrinsic-law intervals need not cover noisy measured labels at 90%; inspect observed PIT/NLL separately. Observed PIT is unavailable for age/distance because their quoted measurement errors are unknown. Source-scale/domain mismatch and posterior-label semantics remain material.

Sample, recipes and limits

Primary references: Teff 688,406; log g 670,065; Fe/H 688,406; alpha 1,707,545; age 161,359; distance 163,887. Features: 2,460,108 complete-XP; 34,206 official no-XP; 1,918,634 publication-unknown photometry and 34 published-XP/not-staged cases are diagnostic. These counts do not mean 4.41M fully labelled primary stars. All current FIT/TUNE/CAL and known ancestor exclusions are retained; full earlier-parent history is not certified.

Each training run completed 450M reconstruction presentations (three passes of the same 150M unique training cache), initialized from the c544 4.5B parent. TUNE-selected states: quantile 450M, Gaussian 150M, both NSF 50,003,968 presentations. Shared reconstruction weight 0.03 and 25% supervised whole-XP dropout. No separate label-order ranking, CRPS training term, Chang tri-loss or spectral-consistency term. Mixed uses per-label NLL + 0.1 InfoNCE with both contexts noise-convolved; it is not a separately trained ContrastiveFlowConformal wrapper. All density heads factorize conditional coordinates.

Age/distance are StarHorse posterior-reference labels, not identified physical truth; alpha uses the declared DESI scale, with Fe/H below −2 not recommended as physical alpha truth. External APOKASC ages are separate and include source values above 60 Gyr under the positive ordered-bound selection; no physical-age upper-limit/quality-flag filter was applied. Treat the external aggregate as a source-scale diagnostic.
