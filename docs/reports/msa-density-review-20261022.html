Metric availability and exclusions

Definitions, units and evaluation policy

Point: torchregress MAE, MSE, RMSE, median absolute error, signed bias, scaled residual MAD (=NMAD here), R², Huber loss (δ=1 in the stated native unit), 10% trimmed MSE, prediction-on-label attenuation slope, normalized RMSE (target standard deviation/IQR), absolute-mode 3σ outlier fraction. Pearson is an auxiliary torch correlation.

Distribution: mean negative log density, analytic Gaussian CRPS where applicable, Dawid–Sebastiani moment score, PIT KS and descriptive 20-bin χ²/Cramér–von Mises, mean pinball across the archived/analytic quantile levels, predictive central coverage/width/interval score at available levels. A five-quantile pinball score is not full CRPS.

Joint NLL: natural logs per object after the native-unit Jacobian. Three-label joint scores have units K,dex,dex and cannot be numerically compared to scalar FeH log scores. Intrinsic and measurement-convolved observed densities are separate forecasts.

Intervals: central predictive quantiles, marginal 90% mean-residual CP, and Bonferroni simultaneous-90% box components are distinct. Density-level-set CP is a joint nonrectangular set with coverage; no rectangle width or set volume is invented.

Verification: “verified means/CP; density FAILED” retains independent mean and calibration checks while preserving the failed score replay. “Producer only” has not passed a full independent role replay. A current metrics re-evaluation does not change old training/library pins or clear old failures.

Limits: single seeds except the fixed three-seed ensemble, repeated test use, measurement-label systematics and unsupervised pretraining overlap. Target errors are diagonal quoted measurement uncertainties; they are not full physical-truth covariance. Long and frozen studies have different budgets.
