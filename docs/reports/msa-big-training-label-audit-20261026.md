# Big-training label audit and next-experiment fixes

Audit date: 26 October 2026, America/Vancouver. Scope: the six executed targets, their local source artefacts, actual four-arm training path, preserved role manifests, independent source-only counts and current publisher documentation. This is an audit/review, not a new training run or a fresh all-row re-verification of every raw catalogue. No jobs, model/checkpoint execution, catalogue acquisition or scheduled work was started. Older source proofs are explicitly reused, not silently rerun or upgraded into physical-truth certificates.

## Executive decision

The current four-arm wave is not a suitable final six-dimensional posterior experiment. It combines a small inherited supervised FIT, source-specific labels with different dependencies, and factorized Gaussian/scalar-flow heads. More reconstruction epochs or a different scalar density loss will not repair those design limitations.

Two distinct defects are visible in the corners. First, the actual Gaussian/scalar-NSF heads cannot learn within-star cross-target dependence: they model the product of six conditional marginal densities at a deterministic embedding. Sharing an encoder does not create sampled covariance. Per-label contrastive likelihood ranking does not change that product. Second, some distributions are confidently centered far from the references. For Gaia 4185355646116022528, the native reference is approximately 4730 K, log g 2.278, Fe/H -3.228, but the Gaussian saved-draw medians are approximately 6476 K, 3.719, -0.615. Its alpha, age and distance references are absent. A more attractive correlation plot would not fix this catastrophic center error.

Before another large run, the release gates are: certify representative failing-star input/label associations; redesign supervised coverage and splits; fix published-source eligibility defects; specify the meaning of all six outputs; establish equal-input supervised baselines; then fit and validate a genuinely dependent joint predictive law. Missing physical evidence must remain missing.

## 1. What actually trained, rather than what was available

The actual model order is Teff, log g, Fe/H, alpha, age, distance. Executed FIT counts are 19,236 / 19,236 / 19,236 / 2,279 / 2,827 / 2,827. TUNE counts are 2,116 / 2,116 / 2,116 / 828 / 1,002 / 1,002; CAL is 2,046 / 2,046 / 2,046 / 490 / 1,024 / 1,024. These come from the five-job preparation manifest, not source catalogue sizes.

Each model completed 450 million reconstruction presentations: three passes over the same 150 million reconstruction objects. This was not 450 million distinct labelled examples. The label sampler balances availability of each target, not gravity, temperature, abundance, survey, sky field or input modality. Native-three labels contribute about 24% of observed-coordinate terms each; log g is not globally underweighted merely because it fails. Alpha contributes about 5%. Counts alone do not establish gradient influence.

The legacy role function requires original benchmark TRAIN membership before assigning FIT/TUNE/CAL. The local native sidecar contains 717,777 observed gravities, but only 27,770 lie in that original TRAIN partition. Hash roles, feature/quality/reference restrictions produce 19,299 native log g FIT stars; cross-target exclusions reduce that to 19,236. Current expansion enlarged TEST while preserving FIT/TUNE/CAL bytes. This was an inherited controlled-comparison restriction, not exhaustion of available labels.

The parent FIT has only seven stars at log g >=4.75 and 25 at Fe/H <-2. Those are upper bounds for the final subset, not exact final counts. It does contain 2,658 stars in log g 4.25–4.5, so empty-bin extrapolation cannot explain every failure there. A complete-case native-three precursor also drops approximately 505 otherwise available FIT Teff/FeH observations. Sparse six-target machinery can retain them separately.

Age/distance preparation first intersects age and distance identities, quality masks and roles, then applies small role caps. It produced only about 2,836 FIT before later exclusions, becoming 2,827 per target. This unnecessarily restricts distance supervision to the age-qualified sample. Earlier selected age/distance cohorts contain about 152,156 / 153,725 distinct objects, with much broader role candidates. Age labels need not exist to train a legitimate distance observation, or vice versa.

Alpha preparation used a 4,607-object feature pilot, then retained its small roles in the large wave. The raw projection contains 3,682,449 eligible measurement rows; the expanded primary evaluation has 1,707,545 source-qualified alpha references. These are not extra training labels, and measurement rows are not distinct stars.

## 2. Teff, log g and Fe/H: native sources and defects

### Executed source definitions

The latest native path uses GALAH DR4 `teff`, `logg`, `fe_h`, and APOGEE DR17 `TEFF`, `LOGG`, `FE_H`, with named per-parameter flags and quoted errors. Teff is in K; log g remains dex(cm/s2); Fe/H is signed dex. Only age and distance are log-transformed in the executed six-target coordinate function. No wrong native log g column, exponentiation, unit conversion or source-ID join was found in this bounded review. Existing original-source audits establish faithful extraction and declared masks, not astrophysical accuracy.

Local native counts are 736,710 observed Teff, 717,777 observed log g and 736,710 observed Fe/H on 756,836 identities. The canonical selection uses GALAH precedence over APOGEE on overlap. It does not fit a survey-offset calibration and does not preserve both as independent likelihood terms in the native-three training view.

Reported precision differs substantially: APOGEE median errors are about 9.13 K / 0.02585 dex / 0.00895 dex versus GALAH 69.71 K / 0.10434 dex / 0.05957 dex. These are not calibrated total cross-survey accuracy. Some quality-selected GALAH gravities have very large quoted errors; a quality flag does not imply an informative label. Across 14,954 eligible same-ID FeH overlaps, the median GALAH-minus-APOGEE difference is only about -0.00445 dex, NMAD 0.0481 dex. Do not blame all failures on a large universal FeH zero-point discrepancy.

### Confirmed selection-policy problem

Our GALAH Fe/H selection requires `flag_fe_h == 0`. The publisher explicitly says this DR4 flag was calculated erroneously and should not be used. The published recommendations also include red-camera S/N >30, `flag_sp == 0`, and per-element flags. The current projection does not impose the explicit S/N recommendation. Fix this as a versioned eligibility policy, preserve the old selections, and recompute the affected identity counts from raw data. The number recoverable cannot be inferred from an already selected sidecar. This is a selection defect, not proof all retained Fe/H values are wrong. [GALAH DR4 guidance](https://www.galah-survey.org/dr4/using_the_data/).

### Existing useful observations not carried into big training

- The selected registry contains 291 older high-resolution Li metal-poor measurements, 276 below Fe/H=-2. The joint-native materializer projects only GALAH/APOGEE; those rows become unobserved in all three coordinates. Recover validated Fe/H sparsely rather than demanding a complete native-three vector. Do not confuse these with the separate Li2025 CaT catalogue.
- Already selected DESI native labels contain 2,976,271 unique stars, including 78,419 below Fe/H=-2 and 3,829 below -3. They were not added to the executed native-three FIT. Teff/log g/metallicity pipeline calibration and domain checks remain prerequisites, not reasons to call every row gold.
- PIGS retains Teff/log g as well as Fe/H from FERRE and ULySS. Source-only finite/error/quality checks find potential FERRE Teff/log g for 11,796 unique IDs and ULySS for 11,650. They overlap and are correlated analyses, not additive independent observations. Current ingestion uses only Fe/H.
- PIGS selected Fe/H has 11,827 unique IDs, including 2,069 below -2; Li2025 CaT silver has 8,304, including 8,288 below -2. Their random errors, method/Ca-enhancement systematics and selection functions must be retained. They are potential tail coverage, not interchangeable high-resolution truth.

## 3. Alpha: a source-specific abundance, not an unspecified universal target

The executed `alpha` target is DESI DR1 RVSpecFit `RVTAB.ALPHAFE` with `ALPHAFE_ERR`, in signed dex. It is not APOGEE global alpha/M and not a GALAH aggregate. The actual native source projection, extension TARGETID alignment, association evidence and signed-unit policy have saved independent proofs. The generic output name hides a much narrower source-scale definition.

Eligibility includes explicit association, spectral success/primary/star flags, S/N and positive finite error. Primary training/evaluation rejects the DESI low-metallicity alpha regime, with raw DESI FeH >-2. Reported metal-poor grouping instead uses native GALAH/APOGEE FeH. Therefore the few alpha references in native-FeH<-2 panels can arise from source disagreement; they do not establish valid physical metal-poor alpha accuracy. Preserve both metallicities and the source validity coordinate. The paper/data model document regime cautions and pipeline differences. [DESI stellar catalogue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14787), [data model](https://desi-mws-dr1-datamodel.readthedocs.io/en/latest/mwsall.html).

There is no executed universal survey-calibration/error-inflation layer in the four-arm SparseSixHead path. An interface in `alpha_heads.py` and design documents are not evidence that source-linked alpha calibration was trained. Per-label Gaussian quoted-error convolution does not model FeH–alpha error covariance or grid-edge/systematic errors.

High-priority extensions already staged are DESI FERRE/SP alpha and PARAM/COVAR in the same combined file; APOGEE DR17 Mg/Si and other named elements with per-element flags; and GALAH DR4 individual X/Fe measurements. Verify actual extension/element order and source IDs rather than guessing positional arrays. GALAH DR4 reduction-only `red_alpha_fe` must not be substituted for a validated analysis alpha composite. Global atmospheric alpha/M, DESI template alpha/Fe and a weighted Mg/Si/Ca/Ti composite are different estimands. A named Mg/Fe output or an explicitly source-linked composite is preferable to silently aliasing them.

## 4. Age: posterior teachers versus anchors

The large six-target runs use StarHorse posterior age medians from the staged APOGEE17/GALAHDR3 posterior products. These are spectroscopy/photometry/astrometry/isochrone-dependent outputs, not independent age measurements. Raw percentile distributions and flags are preserved. The big wave does not use the small joint-teacher pilot objective as its primary age supervision; an implemented pilot is not a large-scale anchored age model.

Actual quality code applies blank output/in-out flags, excludes bad/warning input flags, selects MSTO/SGB ages and relative-width limits. This audit did not find a simple ignored-StarHorse-flags bug. The expanded view additionally demands strictly ordered 16/50/84 percentiles. Source-only checks found 1,112 APOGEE and eight GALAH differences relative to an earlier candidate view; the actual posterior training preparation also applies strict bounds, so those counts do not establish a train/test bug. Staged StarHorse age medians in this check do not exceed 13.8 Gyr.

APOKASC3 is separate seismic/model-based evidence. An independent fixed-width read of all 15,808 local rows matches the whitespace parser exactly for IDs and six age/error columns. The signed-offset parsing repair is real and preserved. However, among 12,301 positive ordered-bound ages, 514 exceed 13.8 Gyr, 240 exceed 20 and two exceed 60 (maximum 66.0861). Four valid entries carry SilverOl; even Gold-only contains 185 above 13.8 and 71 above 20. Thus the spectacular tails are present in the source, not a newly demonstrated column shift. Do not clip them into truth or claim the full positive-bound panel is a clean age benchmark. Use documented stage/quality/model-reliability restrictions, retain alternative solutions and report excluded cases. [APOKASC3 table documentation](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/J/ApJS/276/69/ReadMe).

Willett Kepler/K2/TESS tables are staged. An existing release has a constant Gaia-ID field; audited KIC/APOGEE/TESS mappings are required. Earlier recovery matched 10,081/10,169 Kepler rows, with other fields/missions still unresolved; this is not permission to invent Gaia IDs. Overlapping APOKASC/Willett seismic and spectroscopic teachers are correlated, not independent confirmations. LEGACY provides 66 small main-sequence/subgiant seismic anchor targets with multiple correlated model solutions, not hundreds of independent ages.

Missing useful validation includes carefully matched clusters, detached eclipsing binaries, seismic masses/radii/log g and benchmark stars. These provide different constraints and failure checks, not perfect age truth. DR3 FLAME or XP-learned ages are derived teachers and cannot supply independent XP validation merely through a different catalogue name.

## 5. Distance: the executed target is not a raw-parallax likelihood

The big-wave distance is a StarHorse posterior median in kpc, transformed to natural log for modelling. Posterior widths are deliberately NOT inserted as Gaussian measurement errors. This is correct as far as it goes, but discarding their status as measurement errors does not make the median physical truth. Its teacher has already used spectroscopy, photometry, parallax and priors. Single-median supervision cannot by itself identify a physical distance posterior.

The actual training path does not implement the design document's marginalized raw-Gaia-parallax likelihood. Raw parallax, formal error, zero-point prescription, astrometric solution type/quality and applicable covariance still need an audited production route. Preserve negative/zero parallaxes in that measurement model; do not naively use 1/parallax as a distance label. Declare whether parallax is an input, a likelihood datum, or part of a teacher target, with explicit measurement-assisted controls and no hidden double counting.

Existing Hipparcos/seismic/cluster reference mappings and holdout families are useful external constraints, not a universal exact-distance certificate. Bailer-Jones geometric/photogeometric products and newer StarHorse VACs are valuable comparison/teacher channels but inherit Gaia/model/photometric information. PIGS distances are also model posterior products. Never relabel these independent geometric truth. Age and distance should have independent sparse eligibility masks; their current intersection is a removable pilot restriction.

## 6. Cross-cutting label contracts and leakage

- Current training order is `(teff,logg,fe_h,alpha,age,distance)`; generic reporting schema order is `(teff,logg,fe_h,distance,age,alpha)`. Dedicated executed reports use the training order, so this is NOT evidence of a current plotted-label swap. It is a real adapter hazard: all exports must join by names/units/coordinate tags and test round-trips with distinguishable synthetic values.
- Maintain an observation ledger, not one overwritten scalar per star: catalogue release/pipeline/native field, raw/calibrated value, unit and solar scale, uncertainty type/covariance/upper limit, source row, exact association, quality bits and shared-teacher lineage. Retain alternative observations and estimate links only where overlap supports them.
- Old notebook APOGEE M/H-to-FeH renaming and invented sqrt(age) errors are documented legacy defects. The latest native path repairs Fe/H and rejects posterior widths as measurement noise. Parent86's full prior label/exposure history remains uncertified; do not retroactively certify it merely because current labels are corrected.
- Expanded TEST has been inspected. A new split cannot call recycled stars fresh external validation. Reserve untouched source/family/field holdouts, record all known historical supervised exposure, and distinguish reconstruction exposure from target-label exposure. Cluster/binary/repeat families and correlated teacher pipelines need group-aware handling beyond an integer-ID hash.
- Whole-XP training dropout and input noise are not the same thing. Current label-noise convolution is not propagation of the Gaia XP coefficient covariance. Missing input covariance and unknown label cross-covariance must not be silently declared zero with a physical-posterior claim.
- Current data do not provide broad, independent all-six joint truth. Pair/block overlap, source dependence and missing-not-at-random selection determine which covariances can be learned and tested. Adding a joint architecture alone does not identify every six-dimensional physical degeneracy.

## Missing supporting evidence, distinct from the six target labels

The next source contract also needs measured parallax/error/solution/zero-point applicability; XP coefficient covariance and official publication flags; photometric uncertainties, release and extinction dependence; multiplicity/variability/crowding flags; and evolutionary-state/seismic mass-radius constraints where available. Each must be classified as an input, likelihood datum, nuisance/quality variable or external reference. A useful metadata field is not automatically a seventh ground-truth label. Do not use posterior target distances or ages as hidden inputs when benchmarking their prediction. Context such as sky position can improve population-prior prediction while hiding spatial domain failure, so field/sky holdouts are required for that comparison.

## 7. Newer sources and missing opportunities, checked as of the audit date

**First exploit already staged data.** DESI native-three/SP alpha, APOGEE/GALAH named elements, PIGS atmospheric parameters and retained high-resolution metal-poor stars have lower acquisition risk than another indiscriminate catalogue sweep. New source counts are not automatically quality-qualified, feature-matched, unique or unexposed.

**SDSS-V:** DR20 is public; its new BOSS coverage/VACs are relevant. Official documentation says there is NO new APOGEE analysis in DR20: DR19 APOGEE is carried forward. Audit the newer DR19 ASPCAP analysis rather than counting a duplicated DR20 file as more independent stars. Its calibrated/raw field schema differs from DR17; Teff calibration can use Gaia-XP synthetic photometry and gravities use seismic calibration, so label independence must be recorded. [DR20 scope](https://www.sdss.org/dr20/), [DR19 ASPCAP](https://www.sdss.org/dr19/mwm/astra/pipelines-in-astra/aspcap/).

**LAMOST:** DR12 v2.0 became internationally available in September 2026. LASP and CNN/MRS products are distinct; CNN uncertainty estimates are not supplied by the documented current implementation. Do not equate Fe/H-like metallicity or alpha/M fields to our native targets without an explicit definition/calibration. DR13 domestic/collaborator availability does not establish unrestricted access for our pipeline. [Release notes](https://www.lamost.org/dr12/v2.0/doc/release_note).

**Gaia RVS/GSP-Spec:** useful spectroscopy distinct from XP/GSP-Phot, with detailed flags and parameter/abundance calibration. It is a candidate for atmospheric/alpha validation and coverage, not a drop-in universal Fe/H/alpha label. Gaia DR4 is forthcoming, not an acquired new label pool in this audit; release predictions must not be counted as usable rows. [GSP-Spec paper](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243750), [Gaia processing/release context](https://camcead.ast.cam.ac.uk/project_gaia).

**Anchors and weak teachers:** high-resolution metal-poor literature compilations, Gaia-ESO, benchmark/interferometric stars, seismic targets, clusters and eclipsing binaries merit targeted provenance/identity/quality review. SAGA/JINAbase-style compilations require observation-level deduplication and solar-scale handling; counts are not certified here. Pristine CaHK is useful real input/selection and weak metallicity evidence, not high-resolution Fe/H truth. Gaia-XP-derived Shi/Andrae/GSP-Phot or similar learned catalogues can be declared teachers or selectors, not independent XP test truth. Existing Huang/Beers access metadata does not mean a scientific table was acquired. WEAVE/4MOST or any future release is not ready merely because a survey name appears in planning notes.

## 8. Prioritized fixes and acceptance tests

### P0 — mandatory before another expensive run

1. **Audit the catastrophic stars end to end.** For a fixed model-blind sample, bind Gaia ID to raw spectrum/photometry, original XP coefficient order, error/correlation information, published availability, raw labels/flags, actual cache row, parent normalization and final input tensor. Include original-cohort and cache-recovered expanded objects. Compare source-native predictions with an independent bounded implementation remotely if needed. Pass: identical ID/units/scaler/order and reproducible predictions; preserve any failure. Current bounded association checks do not certify every expanded row.
2. **Use actual supervised coverage.** Remove inherited old-TRAIN and comparison caps only under a newly declared exposure-aware split; recover sparse labels rather than demanding complete native-three or age-distance intersections. Pass: per-target unique FIT counts and Teff/log g/FeH/source/modality/sky coverage published, reserved identities disjoint, all available-data claims source-qualified.
3. **Fix source eligibility and target semantics.** Version the GALAH flag/SNR policy; decide alpha definition and keep StarHorse/seismic/geometric channels separate; quarantine legacy aliases/fake errors and document out-of-domain labels. Pass: native-field/flag/unit fixtures and all affected-ID count differences reviewed, no silent substitution or physical-truth promotion.
4. **Make the dependence learnable.** Retain diagonal Gaussian as a marginal baseline, but add a full-covariance or low-rank conditional Gaussian/MDN, then a genuine joint conditional flow/copula where warranted. Sparse labels require a valid observed-subvector marginal likelihood, not filled-in targets or dropping everything without all six. Pass: known correlated synthetic conditionals, analytic Gaussian masked marginals, trained recovery of covariance/coverage, and exact correlated whole-vector sampling.
5. **Correct the information mismatch.** Establish the astrometric provenance/quality route and compare matched phot+XP versus phot+XP+parallax information, including true no-XP controls. Pass: no target-distance leakage, correct measured-parallax treatment/units/zero-point, explicit teacher dependence, same source-qualified split.
6. **Separate task, source and measurement uncertainty.** Decide whether the output is a catalogue-label predictive distribution, latent population conditional, or a measurement-updated physical posterior. Model/fix quoted-error miscalibration and systematic source effects using legitimate FIT/validation overlaps; do not call posterior percentiles independent measurement errors. Pass: native/observed likelihood identities, source-aware uncertainty checks, known/unknown covariance distinguished and demonstrated calibrated coverage in supported domains.

### P1 — controls required before choosing a recipe

7. **Establish equal-input/equal-FIT baselines.** A simple supervised regressor and frozen-encoder head, then fine-tuning, with reconstruction off/on under the same label/input budget. Andrae is an external comparator with different input information, not a controlled architecture baseline. Pass: matched rows/features/optimizer budget and explicit ablation effects.
8. **Measure loss and gradient contributions.** Log per-target supervised, reconstruction and spectral gradient norms/directions, clipping, repeated versus unique labels and rare-bin exposure. Use declared per-target normalization and target/domain sampling. Add spectral, per-label ordering or Chang-style auxiliaries one at a time against the same proper-likelihood baseline. Per-label context-ranking is not label-order ranking or learned six-target dependence. Pass: each proposed term has a finite/gradient test and improves the predeclared validation objective without hiding losses in other regions.
9. **Select and calibrate for intended populations.** Replace an unexplained macro-six half-masked average with declared XP/no-XP use cases and conditional validation constraints/Pareto tradeoffs. Calibrate using TUNE/CAL only; genuine no-XP is not automatically exchangeable with artificial masking. Pass: residual/coverage/PIT/source/domain curves with counts and no TEST tuning.
10. **Evaluate the joint law and applicability.** Use joint log scores where a valid measured subvector law exists, energy/variogram and conditional checks on observed blocks, joint-region coverage where supported, and tail/input-OOD flags. Marginal PITs and CRPS cannot validate a copula. Unknown or tiny-reference groups stay unscored/diagnostic. Pass: same-object reference/source masks, independent proper-score arithmetic and no unsupported six-target accuracy claim.
11. **Fix export/sample contracts and plots.** Preserve target names/order, log-coordinate Jacobians, observation masks and whole correlated sample vectors; verify round-trips. Keep common axes, source IDs, modality, reference uncertainty and per-star failure flags. Never shuffle/recolour independent draws to manufacture correlation. Show successful, ambiguous and failing cases under a model-blind selection rule. Pass: sampler covariance matches the actual head, no clipped mass hidden and no invented PDF/PIT for the three-quantile baseline.

## 9. What remains unproved

A schema/order risk is not an established current label swap. A publisher flag defect is not proof every retained label is wrong. Correlated residuals across heads do not identify one training-loss cause. A positive-definite joint head will not create missing physical information, and physical correlation should not be drawn just because it looks plausible. Source-row/hash fidelity is not calibrated astrophysical truth. Candidate newer catalogues have not been acquired, merged or quality-counted here.

Current schedules remain deleted. This is a finite review and fix list, not authorization or an automatic launch of another experiment wave.
