# Common reference and density formulation

The current candidate uses a **six-dimensional conditional neural spline flow** from the pinned torchregress implementation. It is not a Gaussian prediction head: its Gaussian base is transformed by a context-dependent nonlinear invertible spline flow. Gaussian and mixture heads remain comparison controls. The unused Gaussian importance proposal in the inherited NSF class is frozen and has zero proposal-loss weight.

Let `x` contain the naturally available XP/photometric features and signed measured parallax, using the pretrained feature normalization. The encoder gives `z = E(x)`. A whole six-vector `u` is sampled from `q_phi(u | z)`. Its coordinates are standardized Teff, logg, Fe/H, alpha/Fe, ln(age/Gyr), and ln(distance/kpc), with the original FIT-only target normalization retained.

A survey observation is modeled through `y_s = M_s h_s(u) + epsilon_s` in the declared target coordinates. `M_s` selects the quantities that source actually provides. `h_s,j` is currently an own-coordinate offset, positive affine transform, or continuous monotone piecewise-linear spline with positive tails. Maps act on each predictive draw exactly; no Taylor propagation is used. They support all six coordinates where labels exist. They do **not** yet represent a gravity correction that varies independently with temperature, metallicity or observing conditions.

The gauge is explicit: APOGEE defines temperature and iron, DESI defines alpha/Fe, and the APOGEE StarHorse posterior-median convention defines age and distance. Gravity has an identity observation edge to FIT-only APOKASC seismic labels. These conventions and the relative survey maps do not establish an unbiased physical reference for every population. The old equal-weight zero-sum convention was an arithmetic coordinate choice, not an absolute calibration.

The optimization objective is a sum over source observation records: masked energy score, optionally plus marginal CRPS, source-map regularization, and masked MAE reconstruction at weight 0.03. Measurement-noise draws are added after source projection. Quoted errors enter the available coordinates. Correlation and covariance-matched t4 controls apply to complete known-error APOGEE/GALAH atmospheric blocks. Unknown-error floors are operational assumptions, not measured uncertainties. Posterior-teacher widths are not silently treated as independent measurement errors.

The source-wise sample score avoids the unstable missing-target importance integral used in the earlier NSF NLL experiment. A full-vector NSF density does not make a noisy missing-label integral exact. Marginal predictions and intervals can instead be estimated from genuine whole-vector samples. CAL interval thresholds are a separate product and do not change or physically certify the density.

**Dependence limitation:** the mapped-source objective scores each source subset separately. Cross-block correlations not jointly observed/scored in a record are not newly identified by that objective, even when different records belong to the same star. The warm-start pooled-label flow had some cross-target supervision, so such dependence is inherited and can change through shared parameters; it is not a new calibrated physical covariance measurement. Single-star corners display actual coupled model draws, including this uncertainty in interpretation. They must not be generated by independently pairing six marginal draws. A grouped same-star observation score or validated posterior-sample distillation would be an additional experiment, not an implicit claim of the current marginal-first candidate.

## Final source and launch decision

The verified full-raw source supersedes the smaller parent cohort for the large fit: **7,693,002 labelled FIT stars**, **9,203,880 source records per epoch**, and **21,301,186 distinct star-target coordinates**. Separate roles contain 1,041,809 TUNE, 804,075 CAL and 883,819 DEV stars. There are 10,427,497 usable-input stars across admitted roles; registry-only/excluded rows are not counted as usable stars. The six FIT target counts are 7,563,233 Teff; 7,642,930 logg; 4,664,047 Fe/H; 1,197,569 alpha/Fe; 115,834 age; and 117,573 distance. Complete six-target support remains 1,281 FIT stars.

The 3,187,386 additional recovered XP inputs received stable star-level roles and the same benchmark/globular protections. Existing roles, inputs and labels were preserved by identity. All inherited additional-survey labels were independently joined back to their native catalogues with exact signed int64 Gaia IDs and matched; the audit found no integer/float identity corruption in these sources.

After ESA returned 503s, saved valid responses were retained. A 512-star overlap check established agreement in parallax, formal error and null status with the [GAVO Gaia DR3 table](https://dc.zah.uni-heidelberg.de/tableinfo/gaia.dr3lite). The remaining recovery used that verified mirror. In total, 34,179 of 34,206 queried parent-cohort gaps had usable measurements. Response hashes and provider-specific provenance are retained; no native spectroscopic labels or positions were sent.

The paired mean TUNE scores are 0.1460166 for energy-only and 0.1461562 for energy plus CRPS, a difference below 0.1%. This is a near tie with two seeds, not decisive superiority. The operational choice is energy scoring, a six-target NSF, and the seismic-anchored monotone spline map. The tested fixed positive/negative correlation, t4 and smaller unknown-error floor did not improve the matched baseline TUNE score. That does not identify measurement covariance or prove all source errors independent.

The reconstruction stream retains MAE weight 0.03 and now samples contiguous blocks across the entire 150-million-row cache with an independent RNG, rather than repeatedly using a prefix. Latest training checkpoints retain optimizer state, flow/noise/mask/global RNG states, reconstruction RNG state and the epoch-order initial RNG state for recovery. Every admitted non-anchor FIT observation record is visited once per full epoch; sparse teacher/seismic auxiliary presentations are logged separately.

## Seismic reference caveat clarified after launch

The seismic identity edge is a convention, not a claim of an unbiased absolute gravity scale. The current run explicitly supervises 5,321 FIT APOKASC stars, repeatedly drawing 32 anchor records per joint update. Its quoted per-star errors do not provide an explicit learnable common-mode calibration uncertainty. The running experiment has not been changed in place.

APOKASC-3 states that its reported seismic gravities omit the f_nu_max correction (Table 4 discussion, footnote 52). Its observing cadence and analysis methods also matter near the measurement limits. See [Pinsonneault et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4365/ad9fef). These facts support investigating reference-scale systematics; they do not establish that such systematics cause the present MSA residuals.

A proposed comparison should treat each seismic catalogue/pipeline as a noisy observation channel, with constrained calibration departures and a source-level systematic component. A shared source offset is not independent per-star scatter and does not average down with star count. If seismic and spectroscopic mappings are all free, an explicit reference convention or external constraints remain necessary to identify common logg. FIT-only fitting and whole-catalogue/pipeline holdouts should supplement star-level checks. No new mapping or matched seismic-on/off experiment has yet been launched by this clarification.
